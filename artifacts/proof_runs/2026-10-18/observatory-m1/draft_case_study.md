# Draft — When the observer fails, preserve what it actually saw

Private engineering draft; not approved for publication.

A controlled reliability corpus exposed lost TLS detail, incomplete failure receipts and a GET abort race. Repairs preserve stage measurements, keep address attempts separate, and explain HTTP method coverage without calling it an outage. The initial 70/72 result and repaired 72/72 regression result are both retained.

A separate fixed synthetic comparison found no Sentinel precision or recall advantage over its operational gate. EWMA and CUSUM results, false incidents and uncertainty remain visible. This is a useful negative result, not a product claim.

No customer targets or payloads are included. These local experiments do not prove unattended operation, incident delivery, two-tenant isolation, independent observation points, seven-day coverage, security detection or commercial readiness.
